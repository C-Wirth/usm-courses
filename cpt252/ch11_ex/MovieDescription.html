Text Description of Movie Review

Premiering in December of 2001, The Fellowship of the Ring follows a Hobbit's call to adventure in order to defeat the Dark Lord Sauron.   Who would have thought that a young hobbit from the Shire could possess the fortitude to embark on such a journey?   Well Frodo is no ordinary hobbit, as he is a Baggins, nephew to renowned adventurer Bilbo Baggins.   Now under the tutelage of Gandalf the Gray, Frodo and his companions begin their mission to save the Middle Earth from the wrath of the Dark Lord Sauron.
