Text Description of Album Review

Okay, Today we are reviewing Lynyrd Skynyrd's second studio album: Second Helping.  Released in April of 1974, this album features their most famous song: Sweet Home Alabama.   The 1974 release featured eight songs, my favorite being Call Me the Breeze - which was actually a cover written by J.J. Cale two years prior.  Call Me the Breeze features great guitar riffs, a catchy course, and a fun upbeat tempo great for any casual event.   Additionally, some other notable songs include The Ballard of Curtis Lowe, Swamp Music and I Need You.   In 1997 there was a CD reissue that included three additional bonus tracks!   In the end, Second Helping reached #12 on Billboard album charts, and was certified Double Platinum in July of 1987.
